Questions people ask

How long does it take to ship from Japan to the USA?

Air parcel takes 6 to 13 days. EMS, used for anything over 2 kg, takes 4 to 7 days. Both are trackable the whole way.

Is it cheaper than paying airline baggage fees?

Usually, yes — and always if it saves you a second checked bag. Most airlines charge $75 to $150 for one extra bag on a transpacific route. Two kilos to the USA costs ¥11,050, roughly $70.

Will I pay import duty?

That depends on your country and what's in the box. Personal goods below your country's duty-free threshold usually clear without a charge. We declare contents honestly on the customs form — under-declaring is what gets parcels seized.

What can't be shipped?

Lithium batteries on their own, aerosols, alcohol above 24%, and anything flammable. Devices with a battery sealed inside, like a camera or a handheld console, are fine.

Why does the price jump at certain weights?

Japan Post charges in 100 gram steps, so a parcel at 1,010 g costs the same as one at 1,100 g. The calculator above shows where you sit on that staircase — if you're just over a step, taking out the packaging padding can drop you back down.
