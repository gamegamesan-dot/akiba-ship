Questions people ask

How long does it take to ship from Japan to the USA?

Air parcel takes 6 to 13 days. EMS, used for anything over 2 kg, takes 4 to 7 days. Both are trackable the whole way.

Is it cheaper than paying airline baggage fees?

Usually, yes — and always if it saves you a second checked bag. Most airlines charge $75 to $150 for one extra bag on a transpacific route. Two kilos to the USA costs ¥11,150, roughly $70.

Will I pay import duty?

That depends on your country and what's in the box. Personal goods below your country's duty-free threshold usually clear without a charge. We declare contents honestly on the customs form — under-declaring is what gets parcels seized.

Do I need to prepay US import duty?

For parcels to the United States, yes. Since the US ended duty-free treatment for low-value mail, anything worth more than about US$100 needs the import duty paid in advance, before we mail it — it is not included in the price above. Personal gifts and documents up to US$100 are exempt. We handle the prepayment process with you. (Last confirmed 2026-08-07.)

What can't be shipped?

Lithium batteries on their own, aerosols, alcohol above 24%, and anything flammable. Devices with a battery sealed inside, like a camera or a handheld console, are fine.

Why does the price jump at certain weights?

Japan Post charges in fixed weight steps, so a parcel at 1,010 g costs the same as one at 1,100 g. Past 2 kg the cheapest option changes from ePacket to air parcel, which is where the biggest jump happens. If you're just over a step, trimming a little packing can drop you back down.
